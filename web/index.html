TrueShuffle

Actually-random shuffles — no clustering, no favorites.

Checking Spotify connection...

Connecting asks Spotify for permission to read your playlists and create your shuffled copies. Your Spotify login and tokens stay in this browser; the TrueShuffle server never sees your account.

Open in Spotify

    Each shuffle writes to a playlist’s own “playlist name TrueShuffle” copy — created the first time, rewritten every time after. Originals are never modified. Changes made in Spotify after this page loads appear after a reload.
